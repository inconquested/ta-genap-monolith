# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indonesian campus organizations (BEM, OSIS, HMJ committees) running formal votes that must be credible, plus general communities, companies, and teams running fast casual polls and collective decisions. Both tiers live in one platform: committees organizing elections where legitimacy is on the line, and everyday users voting from phones on unstable connections.

## Product Purpose

Electa is a real-time polling platform (Laravel 12, React 19, Inertia.js, Pusher WebSockets) for creating polls, voting, commenting, earning achievements, and tracking leaderboards and reports. It exists to replace error-prone manual voting (paper trails, hand counts, opaque results) with verifiable, transparent, instant elections. Success looks like trust plus participation: results nobody can question, and a voting experience light enough that turnout goes up instead of down.

## Brand Personality

Ringan, cepat, seru. A nimble arbiter, not a bureaucrat: confident about integrity, breezy about everything else. Voice is plain Indonesian, active, and specific. Energy comes from momentum (live counts, instant feedback), never from hype copy or gamified noise.

## Anti-references

Generic AI SaaS, first and foremost: purple/blue gradients, the centered hero with three equal feature cards, pill "NEW" badges, and templated landing scaffolding. Electa must never read as another AI-generated SaaS page. Secondarily: neither a stiff bureaucracy portal (dense tables, cold institutional chill) nor a playful quiz game (emoji noise, over-gamification).

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is the feature.** Every screen must answer "was my vote counted, and can I prove it?" Transparency (live distribution, audit trails, verifiable receipts) beats decoration.
2. **Speed respects the voter.** One-click voting, instant feedback, light payloads. Never make a voter wait for choreography or wonder whether their tap registered.
3. **Seru without silly.** Fun means momentum and clarity, not game-show chrome. Achievements and leaderboards celebrate participation; they never undermine election gravity.
4. **Show the count, don't claim it.** Real numbers, real states, real empty/error/loading views. No fake round stats, no dead links, no placeholder promises.
5. **One platform, two tempos.** Formal elections and casual polls share one visual vocabulary; the difference is workflow weight, never a different brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as baseline: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible focus indicators, full keyboard operability, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation. Low-bandwidth mobile is a first-class constraint: cheap Android devices and unstable connections must get fast, legible, usable screens with frictionless operation. No state conveyed by color alone.
